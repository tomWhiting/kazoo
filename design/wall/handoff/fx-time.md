Build rule: every cargo command runs as `nice -n 19 lockf -k /tmp/kazoo-cargo.lock cargo … -j 4`, one at a time.

## Done

- Shared parts are in `time/parts.rs`. Per-effect contract tests are in `time/contract.rs`, generated by a macro. The no-allocation test is `tests/time_no_alloc.rs`.

## Audit fix: noise that rose with the sample rate (done, final run pending)

Causes found and fixed:
1. **`parts::Biquad` ran in f32.** At 192 kHz a low corner (60 Hz lowcut, 40 Hz loop highpass, head bump) puts the poles right at the unit circle, so rounding noise grew with the rate. It now runs in f64 (coefficients and state), with an f32 interface. `parts::flush_wide` was added for f64 state.
2. **`parts::SoftClip` (ADAA tanh) ran in f32.** Its difference quotient cancels badly when each step is small, as at high rates. It now runs in f64 with a 1e-6 step threshold.
3. **`digital` soft-clipped its own input** (about −57 dBc of third harmonic at 0.25 level). Now only the feedback term is clipped: `into = fed + soft(feedback * returned, 2.0)`.
4. **`shift` used Niemitalo's fixed 8-coefficient Hilbert pair**, whose image was only about −49 dBc. It now designs the allpass pair for each rate in `prepare` (`design()` in shift.rs):
   - Polyphase half-band elliptic design (de Soras HIIR closed form), 90° held from 20 Hz, 120 dB target, up to 32 coefficients.
   - The nome is computed exactly through the AGM; the short HIIR series fails for narrow transitions.
   - Sections and the carrier phase are f64.
   - Coefficients alternate: first, third, fifth… go to the quadrature chain; the rest go to the in-phase chain, which runs one sample late.

Measured with `time/rates.rs`, residual after taking out the tone and its harmonics:

| Effect | 48 kHz | 96 kHz | 192 kHz |
|---|---|---|---|
| tape | at the f32 floor | −130 | −124 |
| digital | −131 | −125 | −126 |
| shift | −125 | −124 | −123 |

The others were already the same at every rate (bbd −28 and granular −9 are their images and grain modulation, which don't depend on rate). Level and timing are also the same across rates.

## New cross-rate contract: `time/rates.rs` (registered in mod.rs under cfg(test))

- Sets each effect to a still, linear setting and runs it at 48, 96 and 192 kHz.
- **Level:** within 0.5 dB of the 48 kHz result.
- **Noise:** at most 3 dB above `max(48 kHz result, −120 dBc)`.
- **Timing:** impulse peak within 0.25 ms of the 48 kHz result.
- Prints a table with `--nocapture`.
- The temporary `probe_shift` test has been removed.

## Latency (`Effect::latency`, done)

- **shift**: reports the peak of the envelope of its impulse response, found in `prepare` by running an impulse through the same band filters and allpass pair (7 samples at every rate). The dry path is now held back by the same amount, so a part mix lines up.
- **flanger**: in through-zero mode the dry path is held back by `manual`, so it reports `round(manual × rate)`. It follows the knob and the mode targets. The classic mode reports 0.
- All the other time effects report 0.
- The test `time::rates::latency_is_the_heard_processing_delay_at_every_rate` checks this at 48, 96 and 192 kHz:
  - shift: the impulse peak is within 2 samples of `latency()`, and at mix 0 the dry click comes out exactly at `latency()`.
  - flanger: 0 in classic; in through-zero, the dry click lands exactly on `latency()`.
  - everything else: `latency()` is 0 and, fully dry, the click comes straight through at sample 0 at full level.
